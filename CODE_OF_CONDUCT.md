# Code of conduct

Help make SmilePCB a welcoming place to contribute.

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and different levels of hardware/software experience.
- Explain technical disagreements with evidence and practical alternatives.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against or deliberately humiliate others.
- Do not publish another person's personal information or confidential material.

This applies to the project's issues, pull requests, discussions and other
project spaces. Maintainers may edit/remove inappropriate content, close
disruptive threads or restrict access when needed. They should explain
moderation decisions where doing so is appropriate.

For a sensitive conduct report, use the contact information linked from the
[maintainer's profile](https://github.com/joxerx). Do not post personal details
in an issue. Maintainers are expected to handle reports discreetly and fairly.

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
